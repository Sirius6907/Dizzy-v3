## 2. The five mechanisms that create their experience

### 2.1 Server-driven UI (SDUI) — the biggest one
`assets/appTab.json` (version `cfd7f42`) and `assets/main_operating.json` arrive from CDN and decide:
- bottom tabs: Home · NovelHub · Fight Zone (18+) · Downloads · Me
- Home sub-tabs (14): 🤼FightZone, Trending, Movie, Nollywood, TV, Midnight🔞, Anime, ShortTV, Live, Kids, Education, Football, Music, 😍Free Novels
- every banner, its deep link, its ordering, its artwork

**Consequence:** they can add/reorder a whole tab, swap the home layout, or push a promo — **without an app update**. Dizzy currently hard-codes all navigation in Flutter, so every merchandising change costs a release.

### 2.2 Experiment infrastructure
`assets/hisavana_ab_data.json` — layered experiments (`layer_id`), each with variants carrying `countries / languages / versions` targeting, e.g. the `isSupportDcdn` (dynamic CDN) flag toggled per experiment bucket.
Every UI change they make is measurable before it's permanent.

### 2.3 Hyper-local, not "global app with translations"
- `Banner_Africa`, **Nollywood** tab
- `assets/local_mcc.json` — 225-entry country/MCC table used for regional config
- Transsion OEM SDKs throughout (`com.transsion.*` badge/push/dataservice permissions) — Tecno / Infinix / itel, i.e. the actual African device fleet
- Content tabs tuned to that market: Football, Music, Kids, Education

This is why it feels native to users instead of imported.

### 2.4 Multiple daily-return hooks
Short-form **ShortTV** (vertical drama, with `ShortTVFloatManager`), **Free Novels**, **Live** sports (embedded `sportsnow.top`, FIFA World Cup tag), Fight Zone (18+). Five different reasons to open the app today.

### 2.5 Polish mechanics (cheap to build, huge perceived quality)
- **Lottie gesture coach marks** — `immersion_video_guide_swipe_more.json` animates a hand doing "Swipe up for more"
- **BlurHash + average-hue placeholders** — every image object carries `thumbnail` (blurhash string) and `averageHueLight/Dark`, so images paint a correct-colour gradient instantly and adapt the theme
- **Home-screen widgets** — `HotSubjectWidgetProvider`, `SportSchedulePreviewWidgetProvider`
- **Auto subtitle download** — `SubtitleDownloadManager`
- **PIP / floating window** — `VideoPipManagerImp`, `ShortTVFloatManager`
- Offline downloads with quality + VIP gating (`ResolutionMemberManager`)

## 4. The diagnosis (one paragraph)

MovieBox is a better *experience* while having **fewer features** than Dizzy. It wins because every decision is pre-made for the user and every screen arrives pre-baked from a server. Dizzy hands the user a toolkit: addon → scraper → source → quality → subtitle → player. For a non-tech Indian user who just said "mujhe ye movie dekhni hai", that toolkit is a **tax charged at the exact moment of intent**. 100x does not come from more features. It comes from **deleting decisions and pre-computing everything before the tap**.

## 5. The 100x plan — six moves

### Move 1 · Instant Play (highest impact, do first)
**Goal:** tap Play → first frame. No sheet, no picker, no "choose source".
- Rank sources automatically: measured throughput (Dizzy already has `bandwidth_meter`), last-success score, language match, then bitrate fit for screen.
- Show ONE "Play" button. Source picker only on long-press ("Advanced").
- Pre-buffer next episode 20s before the end; auto-next on by default for series.
- If a source fails twice, silently rotate to next candidate and show a subtle "Switched source" toast — never a modal error.
- **Target:** p50 time-to-first-frame < 4 s on 4G, < 8 s on 3G. Tap-to-play-without-any-sheet > 95 %.

### Move 2 · Config-driven Home (SDUI-lite, not a framework)
**Goal:** merchandising without a release.
- One JSON doc from Supabase: `home_layout_v1` → ordered rails, tab order, banner slots, feature flags per rail.
- Start with **order + show/hide + banner content only**; do not build a general component engine.
- Safe fallback: bundled default layout so a bad payload can never blank the home screen.
- Version it (`v1`) and validate schema before apply.

### Move 3 · Warm start everywhere (perceived speed = real speed)
**Goal:** the app looks instant.
- Persist last ~30 catalog covers + their **blurhash + 2 dominant colours** on disk.
- Paint the shell in < 300 ms from cache; fade real images in.
- Adopt the rival's trick directly: store `averageHueLight/Dark` per image and use it for the gradient placeholder *and* the player background.
- Keep a "stale-but-instant" first frame; refresh in background.

### Move 4 · India-first depth (their Africa move, our India move)
**Goal:** feel made for this market, not translated into it.
- Region rails: Ollywood/Bhojpuri, Punjabi, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Kannada, plus "Hostel Hours" late-night rail.
- Music: desi-first already — add exam-season lofi / driving / sad-night curated rails (Dizzy's existing default addons, not new licensing).
- Language chips **on first run**, not buried in settings.
- Hinglish Easy-English copy pass — rewrite, don't literal-translate.
- Cricket is India's football: live score card + match-day rail. This is the single cheapest retention hook available.

### Move 5 · Return-loop without content spend
**Goal:** reasons to open the app daily that cost ₹0 in licensing.
- Android home-screen widgets via `home_widget`: **Continue Watching**, **Aaj ka Pick**, **Live cricket score**.
- 8 PM local "Aaj ka pick" notification, personalized from watch history (respect existing notification prefs).
- Vertical clip feed: 30–60 s highlight clips from titles already in the library.

### Move 6 · Guided discovery + trust
**Goal:** users learn the app without a tutorial screen.
- Lottie-style coach marks for player gestures (swipe seek, brightness, lock), shown on the **first 3 plays only**, skippable.
- "Why this source?" chip → shows the reason (language, speed, quality). Turns magic into trust.
- Failures in Easy English with a real action: "Doosra source try kar raha hoon…" — never a dead-end modal.

### Phase 4 · Experiment layer (after the above ships)
`feature_flags` already exists — add: stable device-hash bucketing, variant payloads, and country/language/app-version targeting. Then every item above becomes measurable instead of argued about.

## 6. What NOT to copy
- **57 permissions** — calendar, location, camera in a video app destroys trust and invites Play Store scrutiny.
- **Splash ads** — highest-churn moment in the funnel; Dizzy's edge is no-ads.
- **VIP-gating 1080p** — short-term revenue, long-term resentment; Dizzy's differentiator is free + open.
- **`REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES` self-update** — Play policy risk; keep OTA through your own signed flow.
- **163 MB universal APK** — keep the split-only CI guard.
